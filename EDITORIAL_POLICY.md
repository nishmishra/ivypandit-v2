# IvyPandit editorial principles

IvyPandit is an independent scholarly and educational platform.

## Core ethos
**Respect tradition. Encourage curiosity. Demand rigor.**

- Tradition deserves understanding before judgment.
- Scientific claims deserve evidence before acceptance.
- Classical source, traditional interpretation, research hypothesis, scientific evidence, and public commentary should be distinguished.
- Uncertainty is part of scholarship and should remain visible.
- Ancient texts are not automatically modern scientific textbooks.
- Modern science is not the only form of meaningful intellectual inquiry, but empirical claims should be tested empirically.

## Publication labels
Every substantive contribution should be clearly labelled, for example:
- Peer-reviewed publication
- Working paper
- Perspective
- Research proposal
- Scholarly essay
- Lecture/transcript
- Hypothesis or research question

Working papers and perspectives must not be described as peer-reviewed unless they have actually undergone that process.
